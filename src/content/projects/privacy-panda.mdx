Data Principals need frictionless consent control. Data Fiduciaries need auditable evidence. I framed DPDP compliance as those two opposing workflows and designed a dual-portal product on a shared backbone.

With no dedicated engineering capacity, I built the working prototype myself using Claude Code, GPT and Antigravity. The executive team approved it as the build baseline.
